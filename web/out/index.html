How it works

Photo in. Plan out.

No forms to fill in before you see a result. Three steps take you from a single photo to a routine you can start tonight.

  • 01
    Centred · in focus

    Take a photo

    Upload a photo or take one with your camera. Even light, a clean face and a centred, in-focus shot give the most reliable reading.

    • Face the camera, no filters
    • Soft, even light
    • Sharp and centred
  • 02
    7 categories= 100%

    Get your reading

    A fine-tuned image model scores your photo across all seven categories. You see the top result, a confidence percentage, a severity band and the full breakdown.

    • Top category + confidence %
    • Mild, moderate or severe band
    • Scores for all seven

Coverage

Seven categories. Every score, every time.

Each category has its own routine template and its own care rules. Whatever your result, you also see how the other six scored. Tap a card for what it looks like, common triggers and when to see a dermatologist.

  • Not on the list?

    Anything outside these seven is still sorted into the closest category. Read the full breakdown, and see a doctor about anything new, changing or worrying.

General education only. It describes common patterns, not your skin.

What you get

A reading you can actually interrogate.

No black box. Every analysis shows the top category, how confident the model is, a severity band and how all seven categories scored, so you can judge the result for yourself.

  • Confidence you can see

    A single percentage for the top result, plus a bar for every category. Scores spread across several categories tell you the photo was ambiguous.

  • A severity band, honestly framed

    Mild, moderate or severe reflects how strongly the photo matches the top category. It’s a guide, not a clinical grade.

  • Escalation built in

    Severe readings, and moderate ones for rosacea, eczema or hyperpigmentation, come with a clear recommendation to see a dermatologist.

  • Honest about uncertainty

    When no category clearly stands out, the result is marked low-confidence and you’re asked to retake the photo.

Not a diagnosis. SkinSense is an educational screening tool and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. Always consult a qualified dermatologist or physician about a skin concern, and seek care promptly if your skin is painful, bleeding, spreading or changing rapidly.

Routines

Two routines. Led by ingredients, not hype.

Your result maps to a morning and evening routine drawn from 34 widely available drugstore and pharmacy products, organised by step and led by the active ingredients that do the work.

Example routine for a mild acne reading

  • AM · Step 1

    Cleanser

    • Niacinamide
    • Ceramides
    • Hyaluronic acid

    Massage gently for 60 seconds, then rinse with lukewarm water.

  • AM · Step 2

    Exfoliant

    • Salicylic acid 2%

    Apply to dry skin and wait 20 minutes before the next step. Three times a week at most.

  • AM · Step 3

    Serum

    • Niacinamide 10%
    • Zinc 1%

    Apply 2–3 drops to damp skin and press in rather than rubbing.

  • AM · Step 4

    Moisturizer

    • Hyaluronic acid
    • Glycerin

    Apply while skin is still slightly damp to lock in hydration.

  • AM · Step 5

    Sunscreen

    • Zinc oxide SPF

    Apply last, every morning. Reapply every 2 hours in direct sun.

Ingredient view. The app also names an example product for each step, and any product with the same key ingredients does the same job.

Progress tracking

Your own before and after.

Skin changes too slowly to judge from memory. SkinSense keeps your first scan as a baseline and puts it side by side with your latest one, so you compare photographs instead of impressions.

Before / after

Your photos only

These panels fill with your own photos once you sign in and scan. SkinSense never shows you someone else’s skin as if it were your result.

  • 30-day guided plan

    Your plan starts with your first analysis. Every scan after that is logged as a check-in against it.

  • A streak that means something

    Only real activity counts: a scan or a completed routine. Simply signing in doesn’t.

  • Check-in history

    See how the confidence for the condition you’re tracking moves across your recent check-ins.

  • Flags when to see a doctor

    If recent check-ins keep coming back severe, or keep getting worse, your plan recommends seeing a dermatologist soon.

Privacy & safety

Careful with your data. Clear about its limits.

A skin photo is personal. SkinSense stores as little as it can, keeps it private, and is upfront that it’s a screening tool, not a doctor.

Passwordless sign-in
